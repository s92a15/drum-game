# Surface Brief: Game UI & Shell (index.html)

## Direction contract

THESIS:
Classic arcade rhythm game layout, executed with crisp vector geometry and high-contrast color roles, refusing muddy 3D or generic mobile-game clutter so children reading a classroom projector can judge notes instantly.

OWN-WORLD:
Deep navy stage (#1a1d2e), horizontal highway (#2b2d42) with luminous cyan-tinted target ring (#00f0ff). Saturated signal pair: solid fiery orange-red (#ff4757) with dark border for Don (鼓面), sky-blue hollow ring (#1e90ff) for Ka (鼓邊). Crisp rounded corner radius (12-16px), festive golden yellow accents (#ffa502).

STORY:
The student or team looks up at the big screen, hears the countdown beat, tracks notes moving right-to-left along the highway, and strikes the real drum. The hit flashes on the target ring, combo counter scales with excitement, and timing feedback gives instant early/late guidance.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
16:9 arcade canvas centered on screen. Top status bar holds song title, difficulty badge, score (chunky numerals), and gear icon for Calibration/Settings. Upper-middle band is the 120px-tall horizontal note track with left-aligned judgment ring. Bottom-left houses the cheering drum mascot and combo multiplier; bottom-right shows current beat and tempo. Floating overlay modal for Calibration & Microphone Settings.

FORM:
Standard Arcade Rhythm Game (Canon), high-contrast 2D vector kid-friendly aesthetic.

FINISH:
unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
